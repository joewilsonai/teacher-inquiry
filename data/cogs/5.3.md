## Acting on
The culture of the room, or the bond between people in it, named as the thing itself.

## Definition
How the people in the room treat and trust each other, and whether there is a sense of belonging: between students, and between her and her students. It applies when she names the climate or the relationship as the thing (trust, cruelty, warmth, belonging, respect), not when a relationship shows up only as a side effect of something else. The starting place is the culture and the bonds, rather than any one incident.

## Fallback meaning
the starting place is the culture of the room and the bonds between the people in it

## Examples
- When someone gets an answer wrong, the others laugh.
- There are cliques, and a couple of kids are always left out.
- I don't feel like I have a relationship with this class.
- They don't respect each other or me.
- Nobody will share an idea because they're afraid of being made fun of.
- This group just doesn't feel like a community.
- He hasn't trusted me since the first week.

## Near-misses
- "Two boys shove each other in line every day." This is Management, Motivation & Engagement unless she names what is between them as the thing.
- "They talk over me constantly." This is Management, Motivation & Engagement: conduct pulling a response. It becomes this cog only if she says the trouble is respect or trust itself.
- "Group work falls apart because the tables are too close together." This is Time, Space, Transitions & Activities: the arrangement, not the bonds.
