## Cite sentence
Missouri's Standard 5 has no Quality Indicator for this. Instructional Coordination is an authored construct of this system.

## Acting on
Two demands at the same moment, with teaching as one of them.

## Definition
The moments when she is teaching (a small group, a lesson at the board, a conference with one student) and something else needs her at the same time: the rest of the room, a second group, a student at her elbow. The starting place is the holding of both at once, rather than the conduct of the students she is not with, and rather than the layout of the room.

## Fallback meaning
the starting place is holding your teaching and the rest of the room at the same moment

## Examples
- I can't confer with one student without three others lining up at my elbow.
- When I'm working with my small group, the kids working on their own stop.
- I'm trying to teach the lesson and deal with the kid who just walked in late, both at once.
- If I stop to help one table, I lose the other five.
- I can't run guided reading and watch the rest of the room.
- As soon as I turn to write on the board the back half drifts, and I can't do both.

## Near-misses
- "My centers take too long to switch." This is Time, Space, Transitions & Activities: the arrangement, with no second demand on her named.
- "The kids at independent work are off task." This is Management, Motivation & Engagement unless she says the hard part is that she is teaching someone else at that moment.
