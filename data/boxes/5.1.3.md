## Definition
How does redirection happen, and does it work? The starting point here is the teacher's response pattern when behavior drifts.

## Indicators
- The same redirection is repeated many times with little effect
- Redirection escalates in volume or consequence
- The same few students get most of the redirection
- Consequences are inconsistent

## Grade bands
### K-2
Short, calm, private redirection plus praising the behavior you want tends to work better than repeated reminders to the whole group.
### 3-5
Students watch for fairness in who gets redirected and how.
### 6-8
Public redirection can turn into a status contest. Private, low-key correction keeps students from having to save face.

## Examples
- I have to redirect them over and over.
- Nothing I do when they get off task makes a difference.
- It's always the same three kids I'm correcting.

## Near-misses
- "They don't know what's expected." This is 5.1.1.
- "The work is too easy, so they get off task." This is 5.1.2.
