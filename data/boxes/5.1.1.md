## Definition
Do students know exactly what this part of the day is supposed to look and sound like? The starting point here is whether the expectation itself is clear, taught, and the same every day.

## Indicators
- Students ask "what are we supposed to do?" after directions
- Expectations change from day to day or from adult to adult
- Expectations were stated once but never modeled or practiced
- Students do it right for one adult and not for another

## Grade bands
### K-2
Expectations need to be shown, practiced, and practiced again, often with a picture or a signal.
### 3-5
Students benefit from co-creating the expectations and seeing them posted.
### 6-8
Students need the reason behind an expectation and need to see it applied consistently and fairly.

## Examples
- They don't know what they're supposed to be doing during independent work.
- Every day I explain it again and they still ask what to do.
- They're fine with me but completely different with the aide.

## Near-misses
- "They know what to do; they just don't want to." This is 5.1.2: the expectation is clear, so the issue is the work.
- "I redirect the same kids all day and it doesn't stick." This is 5.1.3: it's about how redirection works.
