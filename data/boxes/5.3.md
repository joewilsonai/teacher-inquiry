## Definition
This is about what kind of community the classroom is: how students treat each other, whether they feel they belong and are safe to take risks, and whether the room believes everyone can learn.

## Indicators
- Students are unkind to each other, exclude each other, or tease
- Students are afraid to make mistakes, share answers, or ask for help
- Cliques or groups that won't work together
- Lack of respect between students and the teacher, or power struggles
- Low expectations, or "I'm just bad at this" beliefs
- Conflicts carry over from recess or home into class

## Grade bands
### K-2
Belonging is built through shared routines, classroom jobs, and the teacher's warmth. Conflicts are frequent but short. Tattling and "she's not my friend" are culture signals. Kids mirror the teacher's tone closely.
### 3-5
Friend groups solidify, and exclusion and fairness become big. Students start forming beliefs about who's "smart." Class meetings and shared norms matter.
### 6-8
Peer culture dominates. Status, social-media drama, and identity shape the room. Students test whether the teacher respects them. Psychological safety decides whether anyone participates.

## Examples
- My students are just mean to each other.
- Nobody wants to be partners with two of my kids.
- They laugh at each other when someone gets an answer wrong.
- My kids don't trust me. Everything is a power struggle.
- There's so much drama from lunch that spills into my class.
- Kids keep saying "I'm dumb" and won't try hard problems.
- Tattling all day long.
- The class splits into two groups that won't work together.
- My 8th graders roll their eyes at everything I say.
- Students won't share their writing because they're afraid of being made fun of.

## Near-misses
- "They won't raise their hands to answer questions." This is 5.1 by default (participation and engagement), but 5.3 if the teacher says it's because they're afraid of being laughed at.
- "Kids talk constantly during work time." This is 5.1: it's engagement with expectations, not relationships.
- "Group work falls apart because they can't share materials." This is 5.2 if it's about materials logistics, and 5.3 if it's that they won't cooperate with each other.
