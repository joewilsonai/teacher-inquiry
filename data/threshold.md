# The threshold: what she sees when an account sits beyond classroom management

When what a teacher wrote sits beyond classroom management, she sees one sentence saying where
it sits, and the turn stops there. Each section below is one of those places.

- `status:` is `final` when the sentence is Kim's own words, otherwise `DRAFT` and who wrote it.
- `sentence:` is exactly what the teacher sees. It can run onto more than one line; leave a
  blank line after it.
- `lookup:` is the same kind of status, for the paragraph underneath. That paragraph says what
  sits in that place. The teacher never sees it. It is how the system tells the places apart,
  so a sentence can be final while its paragraph is still a draft.

To add a place, add a section. The heading is its short name (one word, lower-case).

## curriculum
status: final
sentence: That one sits in curriculum, not classroom management — this system only works inside classroom management.
lookup: DRAFT by Luna

The content and how it is taught: what to teach, in what order, with what materials, and how to explain it. Unit and lesson plans, lining plans up with standards, pacing guides, how to teach a particular concept or skill to a group.

## crisis
status: final
sentence: That one needs your building's crisis protocol, not this.
lookup: DRAFT by Luna

A violent or dangerous episode, or what follows one: someone hurt or at risk of being hurt, furniture thrown, a room cleared or evacuated, a student in crisis. Also an account where she says the ordinary procedures do not touch what is happening.

## parents

sentence: That one sits with a parent, not inside your classroom — this system only works inside classroom management.
lookup: DRAFT by Luna

What a parent or family member does, says, wants or fails to do, and how to answer them: an angry email, a call that is not returned, a conference, a complaint.

## atypical
status: DRAFT by Luna
sentence: That one isn't yours to move with classroom management — this system isn't built for a room in that condition.
lookup: DRAFT by Luna

A classroom whose standing condition is outside what this system was built for: most of the day is about keeping people physically safe, or the room is a specialized setting running without the support it is meant to have. The room as a whole, day after day, not one episode.

## elsewhere
status: DRAFT by Luna

lookup: DRAFT by Luna

Anything else that is not hers to move from inside her own room: the culture of the building, what administrators or colleagues do, staffing, pay, policy, her contract, her health.
